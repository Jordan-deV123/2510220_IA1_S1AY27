Jump to: 7 Cures for a Lean Purse | 5 Laws of Gold

Book source: The Richest Man in Babylon PDF

1. Seven Cures for a Lean Purse

The chapter “Seven Cures for a Lean Purse” teaches me that building wealth takes time and that I need to be careful with how I spend my money. The first lesson I learn is saving at least 10% of the money I have before spending the rest. As a student, I have to think financially about how to use the money I have because there are a lot of things I need to buy and others I just want but that are not necessary. The chapter also talks about planning for the future, investing wisely, and finding ways to earn more money. I may not be able to do all these things right now, but I can start by learning new skills and being more responsible with my spending. I can apply this lesson by saving a little money every time and trying not to spend it on other things that I don’t really need right now. This chapter also made me realize that I don't have to wait until I get a job and earn a lot of money before I start saving. I can start with what I have now.

2. Five Laws of Gold

The chapter “Five Laws of Gold” teaches me that having money is not enough; if I do not know how to use it properly, I could lose it. I learned that I should save money, look for ways to grow it, listen to people who understand finances, and avoid investing in things I do not know much about. I also learned that I should be careful with people who promise to make me rich quickly because I could end up losing my money. As a student, I may not have much money to invest right now, but I can still learn how to make better financial decisions for the future. For example, if I decide to invest when I start earning money, I should do my research and understand the risks before making a decision. I should not follow others just because they invest large amounts of money or make it seem like earning a profit is easy. Some people may be scammers who use these promises to gain my trust and steal my money. I also need to control my desire to get rich quickly because it could cause me to ignore the risks and make decisions I might regret later. This chapter reminds me that it is better to take my time, think carefully, and make wise decisions than to rush into something just because I want to make money fast.

Conclusion

In the end, I learned that financial success is not just about how much money I have, but how I decide to use it are two different things. Before reading the book, I thought that having more money would make life easier, but now I realize that if I do not know how to manage it, I could still end up struggling financially. As a student, I may not earn my own money yet, but I can still start by saving what I can and being more careful about what I spend. I know I will probably make some mistakes along the way, but I believe it is better to learn these lessons now than wait until I start working and have more responsibilities. This book has made me realize that even small changes in how I handle money today can make a big difference in my future.
