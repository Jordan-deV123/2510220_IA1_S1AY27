Top Three Personality Traits

1. Curiosity

When it comes to coding, I am always curious about how a piece of code works. I like to break down each line to understand its purpose and logic. This curiosity helps me improve my coding skills and understand new concepts in my academic life.

2. Creativity

I enjoy keeping my work simple, clear, and easy to understand. I enjoy organizing my code so that it is readable and easy to follow. This creativity helps me present my schoolwork clearly while finding new ways to solve problems.

3. Resilience

When a bug breaks my code, I check it carefully and continue debugging until I find a solution. Resilience teaches me to treat mistakes as lessons rather than setbacks, helping me stay determined when facing difficult assignments.

My Hero / Mentor

Quote:“遇事不决，可问春风；春风不语，既随本心。可我若本心坚定，怎会遇事不决？春风也有春风愁，不劳春风为我忧"。[Original] "You say that when faced with uncertainty, one might ask the spring breeze; if the breeze remains silent, then simply follow one's heart. Yet, were my heart truly resolute, I would not be plagued by indecision. The spring breeze has its own sorrows to bear; there is no need for it to worry on my behalf.” [English Translation]

Background: My hero is Qi Jingchun [齐静春], a character from the Chinese fantasy novel Sword of Coming (Jian Lai) [剑来]. He is a powerful and wise character who stands out to me because of his courage, selflessness, and sense of responsibility. When immortals threaten to destroy the world he lives in, he chooses to stand up and fight against them, even though he could have left and ignored what was happening. His willingness to protect the world and the people in it, despite the danger, is what makes him my hero. My favourite quote is: “You say that when faced with uncertainty, one might ask the spring breeze; if the breeze remains silent, then simply follow one's heart. Yet, were my heart truly resolute, I would not be plagued by indecision. The spring breeze has its own sorrows to bear; there is no need for it to worry on my behalf.”(Qi Jingchun, Sword of Coming, English translation) I admire this quote because it reminds me that hesitation often comes from caring about the choices we make and their consequences. If something truly did not matter to me, I would not spend so much time worrying about it. The quote also teaches me that, although I may seek advice from others, I must learn to make my own decisions and take responsibility for them. Qi Jingchun inspires me to face difficulties with courage and to care about others even when doing so is not easy.

LLMs: Tool, Not a Crutch

The statement "LLMs such as ChatGPT, Claude, and CoPilot must be used as a tool not a crutch" means that AI should support my learning, not replace it. If an LLM writes my code and I don't understand it, I've learned nothing. If I use it to explain an error I'm stuck on, I learn faster.

As a student, I can use LLMs in three useful ways: to explain error messages in plain English, to generate practice problems, and to review code I already wrote for simple mistakes. I must never use them to produce my assignments, because that removes the struggle that actually builds skill.
